Telemetry is disabled

The Curse launcher does not send any telemetry right now. Nothing is collected, nothing is stored, and nothing is sent to any server. There is no retention period because there is no data.

When telemetry is enabled in the future, this page will explain what is collected, how long it is kept, the toggles in launcher settings, and how to request a copy or deletion of your data.
